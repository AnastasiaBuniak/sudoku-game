Privacy Policy

Gummy Sudoku · Last updated: August 1, 2026

Gummy Sudoku (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a soft sudoku puzzle game made for kids and families. It is published as a general audience puzzle game with an expected age rating of 4+ (not in Apple’s Kids Category). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Summary

  • No accounts, names, emails, or passwords
  • Anonymous product analytics (to improve the game)
  • Game progress is saved on your device
  • Occasional full-screen ads via Google AdMob
  • We do not sell personal information

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or intentionally collect:

  • Name, email, phone number, or postal address
  • Photos, contacts, or precise location
  • Account credentials (the App has no login)

2.2 Anonymous product analytics

To understand how the App is used and improve it, we collect anonymous event data, for example:

  • App opened
  • Language selected (English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, Japanese, German, French, etc.)
  • Game mode and difficulty level selected, started, continued, unlocked, won, or lost
  • Whether a move was correct or incorrect (and chances left after a wrong move)
  • Anonymous ad funnel events (requested, opened, closed, clicked, failed) — without advertising IDs

These events are sent to PostHog. We configure PostHog for product analytics without identified user profiles (no “who is this person” profiles). Events may include technical context PostHog or the device OS attach automatically (such as app version or general device type), which we use only to understand product usage—not to sell personal data.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads through Google AdMob at natural breaks after a win or game over (for example when the player continues, starts the next puzzle, or leaves the end screen). Ads are not shown on top of the win celebration.

Google and its partners may collect or receive information used to serve and measure ads, which can include:

  • Device and app information (such as device type, OS version, app version)
  • IP address and general (non-precise) location derived from it
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), where available and permitted
  • Ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or closed)

We configure ads with a family-appropriate maximum content rating. How Google uses data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob help.

2.4 Data stored on your device

The App stores locally on your device:

  • Unfinished puzzle progress (so you can Continue later)
  • Level unlock progress and win counts
  • Language preference
  • A short-lived flag used only to show a pending end-of-game ad at the right moment

This local data stays on the device unless you clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. It is not uploaded as a personal profile.

3. How we use information

  • Improve difficulty, onboarding, and language coverage
  • See which levels and features are popular
  • Understand mistakes vs. successful plays to balance the game
  • Show occasional ads to support the App
  • Fix bugs and monitor overall health of the App

We do not sell personal information.

4. Advertising practices

  • Ads are full-screen interstitials at natural break points (not permanent banners)
  • Ads appear after a win or game over, not during active puzzle play
  • The win celebration is shown first; ads are not overlaid on that moment
  • Content rating is configured for a 4+ / family audience

On Android, you can often limit ad personalization in device settings (Google → Ads). Uninstalling the App stops further ad requests from this App.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is designed to be soft and approachable for children and families, but it is distributed in the general store category (not the Kids Category). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own profiles. Analytics are anonymous product events. Ads are provided by Google AdMob under Google’s policies. If you believe we have received personal information from a child that we control, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

6. Third parties

  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics. PostHog Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — advertising. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — when you download or use the App through them, they may collect data under their own policies.
  • Expo / EAS (build infrastructure) — used to build and distribute the App; not used to profile end users inside gameplay.

7. International processing

Analytics and advertising data may be processed on servers in regions used by PostHog and Google (for example US or EU). By using the App, you understand that anonymous analytics and ad-related data may be processed in those regions.

8. Your choices

  • Uninstall the App to stop new analytics and ad requests from this device
  • Clear App storage (or uninstall) to remove local progress and settings
  • Change language anytime in the App (globe icon)
  • Adjust device ad / privacy settings where your OS provides them

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
