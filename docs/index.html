Privacy Policy

Gummy Sudoku · Last updated: July 24, 2026

Gummy Sudoku (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a soft sudoku puzzle game made for kids and families. It is published as a general audience puzzle game with an expected age rating of 4+ (not in Apple’s Kids Category). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Summary

  • No accounts, names, emails, or passwords
  • Anonymous product analytics only (to improve the game)
  • Game progress is saved on your device
  • No ads in the current version
  • Ads may be added later in a family-friendly way; we will update this policy first

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or intentionally collect:

  • Name, email, phone number, or postal address
  • Photos, contacts, or precise location
  • Account credentials (the App has no login)
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID) for product analytics

2.2 Anonymous product analytics

To understand how the App is used and improve it, we collect anonymous event data, for example:

  • App opened
  • Language selected (English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, Japanese, German, French, etc.)
  • Difficulty level selected, started, continued, unlocked, won, or lost
  • Whether a move was correct or incorrect (and chances left after a wrong move)
  • When a full number (1–9) is completed on the board

These events are sent to PostHog. We configure PostHog for product analytics without identified user profiles (no “who is this person” profiles). Events may include technical context PostHog or the device OS attach automatically (such as app version or general device type), which we use only to understand product usage—not to sell personal data or build ads profiles.

2.3 Data stored on your device

The App stores locally on your device:

  • Unfinished puzzle progress (so you can Continue later)
  • Level unlock progress and win counts
  • Language preference
  • Mute / sound preference

This local data stays on the device unless you clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. It is not uploaded as a personal profile.

3. How we use information

  • Improve difficulty, onboarding, and language coverage
  • See which levels and features are popular
  • Understand mistakes vs. successful plays to balance the game
  • Prepare for future features (such as when a number is completed—possible future ad break)
  • Fix bugs and monitor overall health of the App

We do not sell personal information.

4. Advertising (current and future)

Current version: the App does not show advertisements.

Possible future update: we may introduce family-friendly / contextual ads, for example after a player fully completes a number (all placements of a digit), with limits so ads are not shown constantly. If we add ads, we will:

  • Update this Privacy Policy before ads go live
  • Update Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data safety disclosures
  • Configure ads in a manner appropriate for a 4+ / family audience

5. Children’s privacy

The App is designed to be soft and approachable for children and families, but it is distributed in the general store category (not the Kids Category). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Analytics are anonymous product events. If you believe we have received personal information from a child, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

6. Third parties

  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics. PostHog Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — when you download or use the App through them, they may collect data under their own policies.
  • Expo / EAS (build infrastructure) — used to build and distribute the App; not used to profile end users inside gameplay.

7. International processing

Analytics may be processed on servers in the region configured for our PostHog project (for example US or EU cloud). By using the App, you understand that anonymous analytics events may be processed in that region.

8. Your choices

  • Uninstall the App to stop new analytics events from this device
  • Clear App storage (or uninstall) to remove local progress and settings
  • Change language anytime in the App (globe icon)

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes (including if ads are added). The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
